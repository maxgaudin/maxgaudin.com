About

I'm a New Orleans-born technologist, entrepreneur, and civic leader with more than 15 years of experience building organizations and technology products.

I helped establish and scale Operation Spark into Louisiana's largest software engineering school, where I currently serve as Chief Operating Officer. My work has included organizational leadership, strategic partnerships, fundraising, workforce development, and building systems to support growth.

I've also co-founded businesses, served as CTO of a Techstars-backed startup, and advised organizations on technology strategy, software development, and operations.

In recent years, much of my work has focused on Louisiana politics. Through Burn It Down PAC, I develop civic technology, political data platforms, and digital infrastructure while advising campaigns and political organizations on digital strategy.

My current work focuses on making government, elections, political money, and public data easier to understand and use.
